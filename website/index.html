01 / Asymmetric Patience

Why breadth serves patience

Waiting on your hands is easy when you see the whole field and know nothing worthy is cheap. Scarcity of ideas—not scarcity of market noise—causes bad decisions.

Habitual Manual Workflow

Narrow focus, noisy urgency

  • Attention Trapped in 5–10 familiar tickers; forced to swing at mediocre pitches.
  • Numbers Hand-copied spreadsheets and management-adjusted EPS that hide true dilution.
  • Cadence Continuous ticker checking that turns long-term compounding into anxiety.
  • Argument Echo-chamber AI summaries that flatter your existing conviction instead of testing it.

The M3 Posture

Quiet breadth, surgical conviction

  • Attention Indexes every US issuer calmly on your own machine—strictly Circle-gated Strike alerts so unknown businesses stay Quant Screens until you study their industry.
  • Numbers Audited, deterministic 5Y cash-return accounting + inline SEC 10-K Business & Risk profiles directly on every signal card.
  • Learning Industry Research Studio powered by Embedded Alan (4 parallel lenses + bounded Web Search + Feynman Mastery check) expands your Circle safely.
  • Argument Four independent adversarial lenses—Moat, Accounting, Peers, Pre-Mortem—before you write a single judgment.

03 / First Principles

Four rules that never bend

Before committing to Munger value research, M3 ran and audited short-horizon trend and carry strategies against out-of-sample data—and archived both in the open after negative real-world results. Discipline starts by respecting reality.

RULE 01

Arithmetic Is Sacred

Never ask a language model to do accounting. Returns on invested capital, strict Owner Earnings (deducting real capex and stock-based compensation in full), share dilution, and reverse-implied growth are solved deterministically from official primary filings—with hash provenance down to the filing fact.

Zero-LLM financial math · Primary filing traceability
RULE 02

Invert, Always Invert

Munger's favorite trick is asking where a thesis dies before calculating what it might pay. Every deep-research run tests competitive moat, accounting reality, structural lookalikes, and a dedicated Pre-Mortem—surfacing falsification criteria instead of sales pitches.

Four adversarial lenses · Hard Too Hard veto gate
RULE 03

Sovereignty Belongs to the Owner

Software can widen your peripheral vision and challenge your blind spots, but it cannot carry your conviction through a 40% drawdown. M3 structurally blocks itself from outputting buy or sell advice; strike prices and thesis weights remain strictly human-authored.

Policy-enforced boundary · Append-only Decision Journal
RULE 04

Sit Calmly, Spend Almost Nothing

Great compounding hates frantic overhead. M3 runs locally on your Mac with zero standing cloud infrastructure, zero broker keys, and hard-capped research spend. Routine screening costs nothing; deep interrogation only runs when facts earn it.

Local-first · Hard budget gate · Paper research discipline
