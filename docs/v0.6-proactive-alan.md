# Project M3 `v0.6.0` — Proactive Alan Design & Reference

> **Status**: Implementation specification & architecture reference for `v0.6.0` (strictly **Branches A + B + C**).  
> **Related**: [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) · [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) · [docs/munger-workflow.md](./munger-workflow.md) · [docs/agent-interface.md](./agent-interface.md) · [docs/mcp-server.md](./mcp-server.md)

`v0.6.0` evolves **Alan** inside Project M3 from an on-demand responder into a calm, bounded, human-sovereign Munger analyst across three focused branches:

- **Branch A — L1.5 Circle Filing Watchdog**: Low-token filing triage strictly scoped to `WATCH` (`待审讯`) and `IN` (`核心击球`) Circle companies against pre-registered falsification criteria.
- **Branch B — Calm Daily/Weekly Briefings + NSH Native Background Keep-Alive**: Single-process macOS Menu Bar Tray keep-alive (`AppOptions::keep_alive_on_close` + `SetTray`) powering deterministic 3-part Circle briefings with 0-token quiet periods.
- **Branch C — Interactive 3-Round Socratic Feynman Coach & `WATCH → IN` Soft Gate**: Text-first Socratic defense drills (compatible with macOS native Dictation) in Company Desk and Industry Studio, backed by atomic `human_judgment` sign-off and a `WATCH → IN` graduation soft gate.

Multi-market expansion (**`HKEX`** filing ingestion, dual-market data pipelines, and multi-currency handling) is intentionally deferred to **`v0.6.5`**.

## 1. Design Decisions (`Q1–Q9`)

| ID | Question / Area | Decision in `v0.6.0` | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1** | **Briefing & Event Inbox IA (`⌘1`)** | Keep `⌘1` as a single primary tab with a segmented **`简报 \| 事件流`** (*Briefing \| Event Stream*) switcher. | Prevents top-level sidebar bloat while keeping calm daily/weekly digests one click from raw L1/L1.5 filing signals. |
| **Q2** | **L1.5 Auto-Triage Scope** | Watch **`WATCH` (`待审讯`) + `IN` (`核心击球`)** Circle companies **only**. `OUT`, `TOO HARD`, and non-Circle Discovery signals remain at **0 LLM tokens**. | Aligns token spend strictly with businesses the owner actively studies or owns in their Circle of Competence. |
| **Q3** | **Triage Verdicts, Non-Destructive Noise & Deep Research Boundary** | Classify into `Noise \| Material \| FalsificationAlert` with verbatim primary quotes (`TriageQuote`). **`Noise` is collapsed in UI and never auto-deleted or archived** (`SignalStatus` untouched). **`FalsificationAlert` renders in coral with a one-click 4-lens Deep Research button and is never auto-triggered.** If `human_judgment.falsification_metrics` is empty, fall back to `Dossier.premortem` + deterministic warnings (`fallback_no_metrics_warning = true`), paired with an in-app `falsification_metrics` CRUD editor. | Eliminates silent signal loss and surprise flagship-model bills while making falsification evidence immediately actionable for the human owner. |
| **Q4** | **Background Keep-Alive & DuckDB Concurrency** | Use Longbridge NSH `0.7.0` **`AppOptions::keep_alive_on_close`** + macOS Menu Bar **`SetTray`** inside the single `m3-nsh` process (no separate `launchd` daemon). | Embedded DuckDB (`m3.duckdb`) enforces single-writer/process locking; single-process tray keep-alive enables periodic background catch-up (`background_refresh_hours`, default `4`) with zero cross-process lock errors. |
| **Q5** | **Briefing Structure & Quiet-Period Zero-Token Rule** | Build a **deterministic 3-part skeleton** first (*Strike Radar*, *Filing & Red-Flag Delta*, *Alan L1.5 Watchdog Hits*), then add **at most 1 lightweight Alan Munger-style summary call** only when material items exist (`!is_quiet`). When `is_quiet == true`, spend **0 LLM tokens** and surface `"无球可挥 · 耐心等待"` / `"No Fat Pitches · Stay Patient"`. | Guarantees zero noisy filler when fundamentals and valuations have not moved, and ensures full utility even without a configured LLM. |
| **Q6** | **Feynman Coach Interaction Mode** | Build a **text-first 3-round Socratic coach** inside Company Desk and Industry Studio, compatible with **macOS native Dictation** (`Fn Fn` / *Edit → Start Dictation*). | Avoids brittle custom audio capture/ASR pipelines while supporting both typed and dictated human defenses seamlessly on macOS. |
| **Q7** | **`WATCH → IN` Graduation Gate & Sign-Off** | Moving a company from `WATCH` (`待审讯`) to `IN` (`核心击球`) triggers a **soft gate dialog** (`needs_feynman_graduation_gate`). Completing human sign-off atomically writes `my_stance`, `falsification_metrics`, and `feynman_checked_at`, clears `pending_human_review = false`, and archives the defense summary to Markdown. | Enforces Munger's "earn your right to hold an opinion" discipline without trapping power users behind hard locks. |
| **Q8** | **CLI & Read-Only MCP Exposure** | Add `m3 briefing`, `m3 triage`, and `m3 falsification` subcommands to `crates/m3-cli`, and expose read-only briefing/triage inspection tools via `m3 mcp`. | Keeps external agents (`Claude Code`, `Codex`, standalone Alan) first-class with structured schema-v1 JSON and strict read-only safety. |
| **Q9** | **Proactive Cost & Quota Governance** | Enforce **two stacked gates**: daily proactive quotas (`daily_triage_quota: 10`, `daily_briefing_summary_quota: 1`) **and** monthly hard ceilings (`monthly_budget_usd: $10.00`, `monthly_token_limit: 2,000,000`). Degrade gracefully to 0-token deterministic output if unconfigured or capped. | Makes background and proactive Alan behavior predictable, auditable, and safe against runaway API spend. |

- **DuckDB (`data/munger/lake/m3.duckdb`)**:
  - `signal_triage`: `signal_id`, `ticker`, `verdict` (`noise | material | falsification_alert`), `headline`, `rationale`, `matched_metrics_json`, `suggested_metrics_json`, `quotes_json`, `fallback_no_metrics_warning`, `model_used`, `tokens_used`, `cost_usd`, `created_at_ms`.

  - `workbench/budget.json`: monthly, per-run, weekly auto-trigger, and daily proactive quotas.
  - `workbench/memos/<TICKER>-feynman.md`: appended Company Feynman Defense archives.
  - `workbench/industries/<slug>/feynman-defense.md`: appended Industry Feynman Defense archives.

## 3. Human-Sovereignty Guarantees

1. **Redline Policy Enforcement (`M3ToolPolicyHook`)**: Alan cannot write `human_judgment` (`my_stance`, `weight_rationale`, `falsification_metrics`, `feynman_checked_at`, `pending_human_review`), `watchlist.json`, `journal.json`, `budget.json`, `themes.json`, `memos/`, `industries/`, or `data/munger/` (`m3.duckdb` / `parquet/`).
2. **Explicit Sign-Off Only**: Feynman Coach critiques may return `blind_spots` and `suggested_falsification_metrics`, but `human_judgment` is mutated **only** when the human user reviews/edits and confirms sign-off (`apply_feynman_sign_off`) or directly uses the in-app `falsification_metrics` editor / `m3 falsification` CLI.
3. **Non-Destructive L1.5 Triage**: A `Noise` verdict collapses low-signal cards in the UI while leaving `signal_inbox.status` intact so the user can always expand and audit any filing.
4. **No Auto-Triggered Deep Research**: `FalsificationAlert` highlights breached thesis evidence in coral and surfaces a one-click 4-lens Deep Research trigger; it never silently dispatches L2 multi-agent runs.
5. **Read-Only `m3 mcp`**: All MCP tools carry `readOnlyHint = true` and `destructiveHint = false`; external agents can inspect briefings and triage records without mutating human research assets.

## 4. Daily Quota + Monthly Ceiling Budget Model

`workbench/budget.json` remains backward-compatible via `#[serde(default)]` for existing workspaces:

| Setting | Default | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `proactive_triage_enabled` | `true` | Enables/disables L1.5 Alan auto-triage calls |
| `daily_triage_quota` | `10` | Max L1.5 Circle filing triage calls per day |
| `daily_briefing_summary_quota` | `1` | Max Alan executive summary calls for non-quiet briefings per day |
| `background_refresh_hours` | `4` | Interval for tray keep-alive background refresh |
| `monthly_budget_usd` | `$10.00` | Combined hard monthly USD ceiling (`research_runs` + proactive calls) |
| `monthly_token_limit` | `2,000,000` | Combined hard monthly token ceiling |

Every proactive LLM call must satisfy:
1. Feature enabled (`proactive_triage_enabled == true` for triage; `!is_quiet && allow_llm_summary` for briefings),
2. Active Alan connection configured (`resolve_effective_alan_connection`),
3. Daily quota remaining (`triages_today < daily_triage_quota` / `summaries_today < daily_briefing_summary_quota`), and
4. Combined monthly spend & token usage below `monthly_budget_usd` and `monthly_token_limit`.

If any gate blocks (or Alan is unconfigured), L1.5 triage and briefing generation fall back cleanly to **0-token deterministic behavior** without failing the user workflow.

## 5. CLI & Read-Only MCP Reference

### `m3` CLI Subcommands (`crates/m3-cli`)

All commands emit schema-v1 JSON (`{"schema_version": 1, "ok": true, "data": ...}`) on `stdout`:

- **`m3 briefing`** — Build or inspect Calm Circle Briefings (`daily` / `weekly`), showing the deterministic 3-part skeleton (*Strike Radar*, *Filing & Red-Flag Delta*, *Watchdog Hits*), quiet-period flag (`is_quiet`), and optional Alan executive summary.
- **`m3 triage`** — List persisted `signal_triage` verdicts or run L1.5 Circle Filing Watchdog triage across `WATCH` + `IN` signals (with dry-run / limit controls and strict budget gating).
- **`m3 falsification`** — Inspect and manage a ticker's `Dossier.human_judgment.falsification_metrics` (`list`, `add`, `remove`, `set`) via atomic writes.

### `m3 mcp` Read-Only Tools

Extending the 14 existing read-only tools in `crates/m3-cli/src/mcp.rs` (see [docs/mcp-server.md](./mcp-server.md)), `v0.6.0` adds read-only MCP inspection for:
- **Latest / historical Circle Briefings** (`circle_briefings`) — allowing external agents to read today's Strike Radar, filing deltas, and Alan's executive summary.
- **L1.5 Signal Triage results** (`signal_triage`) — allowing external agents to filter `Noise | Material | FalsificationAlert` records and inspect verbatim `TriageQuote` citations.

## 6. Desktop UX Flows (`apps/m3-nsh`, NSH `0.7.0`)

1. **`⌘1` Segmented `简报 | 事件流` View**:
   - **`简报` (Briefing)**: Displays the 3 deterministic panels (*Strike Radar* for `WATCH + IN` in Strike Zone or `<=15%` above Strike, *Filing & Red-Flag Delta*, and *L1.5 Watchdog Hits*), topped by Alan's concise Munger-style summary when material events occurred—or the calm quiet-period state (`"无球可挥 · 耐心等待"` / `"No Fat Pitches · Stay Patient"`).
   - **`事件流` (Event Stream)**: Displays L1 signals enriched with L1.5 verdicts (`Noise` collapsed by default and restorable, `Material` highlighted with primary quotes, `FalsificationAlert` badged in coral with a one-click **4-Lens Deep Research** trigger).
2. **`falsification_metrics` CRUD Editor & `premortem` Fallback**:
   - Inside Company Desk (`human_judgment` section), owners can add, edit, and delete objective falsification criteria. If none are registered yet, the UI surfaces an indicator explaining that L1.5 Watchdog falls back to `Dossier.premortem` + deterministic financial warnings.
3. **3-Round Socratic Feynman Coach & `WATCH → IN` Soft Gate**:
   - Accessible in both **Company Desk** and **Industry Studio**.
   - Walks the owner through 3 rounds:
     1. *Moat & Unit Economics*
     2. *5-Year Failure Modes & Testable Falsification Metrics*
     3. *Valuation Margin of Safety & Kill Criteria*
   - Text-first inputs work natively with **macOS Dictation** (`Fn Fn`). After Alan's critique per round, the owner reviews/edits `my_stance` and `falsification_metrics` and clicks **Sign Off** (`feynman_checked_at` stamped, `pending_human_review = false`, Markdown defense archived).
   - Promoting a company from `WATCH` (`待审讯`) to `IN` (`核心击球`) invokes `needs_feynman_graduation_gate`; if unchecked or pending review, a calm **Soft Gate** modal offers to launch the Feynman Coach first or proceed consciously.
4. **macOS Menu Bar Tray & Single-Process Keep-Alive**:
   - Closing the main window hides rather than terminates `M3.app` (`AppOptions::keep_alive_on_close`), leaving a clean macOS Menu Bar tray (`SetTray`) with quick actions to reopen the Workbench, inspect the latest Briefing status, trigger a catch-up refresh, or quit cleanly.
