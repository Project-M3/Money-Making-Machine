# Project M3 `v0.6.5` — Unified Alan Design & Reference

> **Status**: Implementation specification & architecture reference for `v0.6.5` (**Unified Alan**).  
> **Runtime Pin**: `crates/m3-core/Cargo.toml` stays on `alan` **`v0.1.4`**, while keeping `/mnt/m3` `FileServer` and policy-hook boundaries forward-compatible with newer `alan9`.  
> **Related**: [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) · [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) · [docs/v0.6-proactive-alan.md](./v0.6-proactive-alan.md) · [docs/agent-interface.md](./agent-interface.md) · [docs/mcp-server.md](./mcp-server.md)

`v0.6.5` unifies every interactive **Alan** conversation, interrogation thread, and 3-round Socratic Feynman drill inside `apps/m3-nsh` into one calm **App-Shell Dual Canvas**—while retiring fragmented per-view chat surfaces, expanding Alan's read-only `/mnt/m3` visibility, persisting bounded scoped sessions atomically on disk, and exposing full-capability CLI (`m3 alan`, `m3 app`), MCP (`m3 mcp`), and live `M3.app` control IPC (`control.sock`).

## 1. Design Decisions (`Q1–Q9`)

| ID | Area | Decision in `v0.6.5` | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1** | **3-State App-Shell Dual Canvas (`Closed \| Split \| Immersive`)** | Render the dual canvas directly on `shell_root_bg()` smoked-black glass with three states: **`Closed`** (100% M3 viewport card, zero chat chrome), **`Split`** (left M3 viewport rounded card + `8px` glass gap + right independent Alan rounded card `~430px` animated via `animate_shell_spring()`), and **`Immersive`** (left nav & M3 card hidden smoothly; centered Alan Studio up to `max_w(760px)` with quick exit back to `Split` or `Closed`). | Keeps Alan immediately accessible alongside live financials without turning M3's calm research desk into a cramped always-on sidebar. |
| **Q2** | **Keyboard Shortcuts & Unified Topbar Toggle** | Add one unified topbar Alan toggle button (with `⌘L` badge) next to Message Box. Bind **`⌘L`** to `Closed ↔ Split` (or `Immersive → Split`), **`⇧⌘L`** to `Split ↔ Immersive` (or `Closed → Immersive`), and **`Esc`** to step `Immersive → Split` before closing other overlays. | Matches familiar IDE/editor split-and-focus ergonomics while preserving one-handed keyboard navigation across the shell. |
| **Q3** | **Global Default `Closed` + Remembered `Split` Preference** | Launch globally in **`Closed`** and keep overview tabs (`Inbox`, `Universe`, `Baskets`, `Journal`) defaulting to `Closed` unless explicitly toggled. Remember the user's open/closed `Split` preference specifically for deep-research views (**Company Desk** and **Industry Studio**). | Overview scanning stays uncluttered at startup, whereas deep company or industry study preserves the owner's preferred working layout. |
| **Q4** | **Auto-Collapse Left Navigation in `Split`** | Automatically collapse the left navigation rail whenever `Split` is open on **Company Desk** or **Industry Studio** (or on narrower window widths). | Protects horizontal breathing room for K-line charts, financial ledgers, and peer matrices without manual sidebar juggling. |
| **Q5** | **Company Desk Tab 2 Retirement & Living Memo Folding** | Retire Company Desk Tab 2 (`2 深度研究与 Alan` / `DeepResearchAlan`), leaving clean **Financials & Living Memo** and **XBRL Inspector** views. Fold the 4-lens Deep Research output into the **Living Memo** section with an async trigger (`生成 / 刷新 4 视角深研`, progress in topbar Message Box) and add lightweight **`问 Alan ↗`** anchors on red-flag alerts, 10-K excerpts, and memo sections. | Eliminates redundant tabs and split mental models: structured artifacts live in Living Memo while live interrogation moves cleanly to the right-hand Alan Canvas. |
| **Q6** | **Industry Bottom Chat & Inline Feynman Unification** | Remove the separate `industry_alan_messages` bottom chat box and inline Feynman Coach block from Industry Studio. Route `打开 Alan 讨论` / `开启费曼答辩` triggers—and both Company and Industry 3-round Socratic Feynman flows—into the unified Alan Canvas segmented switcher (**`审讯对话 \| 费曼答辩`**). | Gives Company and Industry research identical interaction mechanics, Dictation compatibility, and session continuity in one place. |
| **Q7** | **`📌` Context Pin Lock vs. Auto-Follow Scope** | When unpinned, `alan_scope` automatically tracks the active view/selection (`Company(ticker)`, `Industry(slug)`, `Briefing(...)`, `Portfolio`). Toggling **`📌`** freezes the current `AlanScope` so switching tabs or tickers never interrupts an active interrogation. | Supports both effortless "follow my cursor" research and cross-company comparison against a pinned reference thesis. |
| **Q8** | **Inline Deterministic M3 Evidence Cards** | Resolve `[report:TICKER]`, `[xbrl:TICKER]`, and `[dossier:TICKER]` tokens emitted in Alan replies via deterministic `m3-core` (`resolve_inline_evidence_cards`, deduped by ticker) into native inline M3 summary cards showing bucket, latest price, Strike price & gap %, 5Y ROIC, FCF conversion/yield, and warning count. | Grounds Alan prose directly in SHA-256-backed local `rust_decimal` numbers and saved dossiers at **0 extra LLM tokens**. |
| **Q9** | **Collapsed Single-Line Turing Tape** | Collapse Turing Tape entries (`in`, `tool`, `out`, `policy`, `err`) into a single-line expandable status capsule by default inside Alan Canvas, expanding on click to inspect full tool/policy details. | Preserves full `M3ToolPolicyHook` and tool-call auditability without drowning prose in execution logs. |

## 2. `AlanScope` Variants & Bounded Session Persistence

### Unified `AlanScope`

All interactive Alan surfaces share a single typed scope (`crates/m3-core/src/alan_interrogator.rs`):

| Variant | Safe `scope_key()` Example | Auto-Follow Source | Context Preamble Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `AlanScope::Company(String)` | `company-COST` | Active Company Desk or selected company | Ticker financials (`MungerReport`), Dossier draft/redlines, Living Memo, and recent events |
| `AlanScope::Industry(String)` | `industry-retail` | Active Industry Studio slug | `IndustryProfile`, SIC peer matrix, Industry Primer, and Feynman mastery checklist |
| `AlanScope::Briefing(String)` | `briefing-latest` | `⌘1` Briefing / Event Stream tab | Latest `CircleBriefing` (Strike Radar, Filing Delta, L1.5 Watchdog hits) and recent inbox signals |
| `AlanScope::Portfolio` | `portfolio` | Circle Overview (`Baskets`) / Decision Journal | Watchlist buckets (`WATCH → IN → OUT → TOO HARD`), Decision Journal ledger, and Circle-wide exposure |

### Bounded Atomic Session Schema (`workbench/alan_sessions/<scope_key>.json`)

Conversation continuity per `AlanScope` is managed deterministically by `crates/m3-core/src/alan_session.rs` and stored outside `data/munger/` under:

```text
workbench/alan_sessions/<scope_key>.json
```

Key guarantees:
- **Atomic Persistence**: Writes go to a temporary file in `workbench/alan_sessions/` followed by atomic rename—preventing half-written JSON on crash or close.
- **Bounded Turn Window**: Retained turns are capped (last **30** turns) so session files stay fast to hydrate and small in Git/workbench diffs.
- **Graceful Recovery**: Missing or corrupt session files fall back cleanly to an empty session without blocking the UI or throwing fatal errors.
- **Clear Separation from Human Judgment**: Session transcripts never mutate `Dossier.human_judgment`, `workbench/watchlist.json`, or `workbench/journal.json`.

## 3. Expanded Read-Only `/mnt/m3` Mount Tree Contract

`M3WorkbenchFs` in `crates/m3-core/src/alan_interrogator.rs` exposes a strictly read-only (`Access::ReadOnly`) `alan-ap` `FileServer` at `/mnt/m3`. `v0.6.5` expands this mount tree beyond the initial `v0.5` company files so every `AlanScope` (`Company`, `Industry`, `Briefing`, `Portfolio`) can inspect authoritative local artifacts directly over `/mnt/m3`:

```text
/mnt/m3/
├── watchlist.json                # Existing: Circle buckets, valuation multiples, notes
├── journal.json                  # Existing: Paper Decision Journal ledger & ^GSPC benchmark
├── computed/
│   └── <TICKER>.json             # Existing: Deterministic MungerReport (rust_decimal + provenance)
├── dossiers/
│   └── <TICKER>.json             # Existing: Company Dossier (agent_draft + human_judgment)
├── industries/
│   ├── index.json                # New: Study index (slugs, titles, status, bound tickers)
│   └── <slug>.json               # New: IndustryProfile JSON per study slug
├── memos/
│   ├── <TICKER>.md               # New: 4-lens Living Research Memo (+ ## Human Notes)
│   └── <TICKER>-feynman.md       # New: Company 3-round Feynman Defense archive
├── briefings/
│   └── latest.json               # New: Latest CircleBriefing from DuckDB circle_briefings (graceful fallback if empty)
└── events/
    └── recent.json               # New: Recent L1/L1.5 signal_inbox records (capped cleanly, e.g. latest 40)
```

> **Forward Compatibility**: Exposing M3 state through `alan-ap`'s `FileServer` at `/mnt/m3` keeps `crates/m3-core/Cargo.toml` pinned cleanly on `alan` **`v0.1.4`** today while remaining structurally compatible with `alan9` namespace mounts in future runtime upgrades.

## 4. Human-Sovereignty Redlines (`M3ToolPolicyHook`)

All scoped Alan turns—whether triggered from Company Desk, Industry Studio, Briefing, or Portfolio—execute behind the synchronous **`M3ToolPolicyHook`** (`crates/m3-core/src/alan_interrogator.rs`):

1. **Mount-Level Read-Only Enforcement**: `/mnt/m3` is mounted with `Access::ReadOnly`; no file server write operations are exposed anywhere in the mount tree.
2. **Path & Field Redline Inspection**: Any tool argument referencing protected paths or keys is unconditionally denied (`HookDecision::Deny`), including:
   - `human_judgment` (`my_stance`, `weight_rationale`, `falsification_metrics`, `feynman_checked_at`, `pending_human_review`, `industry_status`, `multiple_low`, `multiple_high`, `human_notes`, `feynman_checked`)
   - `computed/`, `raw/`, `data/munger/`, `m3.duckdb` / `.duckdb`, `parquet/` / `.parquet`, `lake/`
   - `watchlist.json`, `journal.json`, `budget.json`, `themes.json`, `memos/`, and `industries/`
   - For `m3_fs_list` and `m3_fs_read`, only `/mnt/m3` and `/mnt/m3/...` read paths are allowed; any host path outside `/mnt/m3` is denied.
3. **Strict Write Allowlist (`m3_update_draft` Only)**: `ToolCapability::Write` is permitted **only** for `m3_update_draft` and **only** for the six draft fields: `ticker`, `three_sentence_business`, `latticework`, `premortem`, `open_questions`, and `draft_source`. Every other write tool is denied.
4. **Network & Unknown Tool Guard**: `ToolCapability::Network` is restricted strictly to `m3_web_search` (`query` + optional `limit` in `1..=8`); all other network or unknown tools are denied.
5. **No Trade Intent or Silent Writes**: Alan cannot emit brokerage orders, mutate `workbench/alan_sessions/` directly via tool calls (session persistence is handled deterministically by `m3-core`), or auto-trigger paid 4-lens Deep Research runs.

## 5. Runtime Fixes, CLI/MCP & Desktop Control IPC

Following live end-to-end qualification of `v0.6.5`, six concrete runtime, CLI, MCP, and desktop IPC upgrades complete the unified Alan surface:

### 5.1 `/mnt/m3` Filesystem Tools & Live Watchlist Bucket Precedence
- **Registered `/mnt/m3` Read Tools (`crates/m3-core/src/alan_interrogator.rs`)**: `boot_m3_embedded` now registers `m3_fs_list` and `m3_fs_read` backed directly by `Arc<M3WorkbenchFs>` (`M3WorkbenchFs::list_mnt_entries` and `M3WorkbenchFs::read_mnt_file_bounded`, implemented on top of `FileServer` operations). Reads are strictly read-only, capped at `M3_FS_MAX_READ_BYTES = 64 * 1024` (`65536` bytes per call with `offset` pagination), and enforced by `M3ToolPolicyHook` so only `/mnt/m3*` paths can be read. `M3_INTERROGATOR_SYSTEM_PROMPT` and all four `AlanScope` preambles explicitly guide Alan to use `m3_fs_list` / `m3_fs_read`.
- **Live `bucket` / `name` Resolution in `resolve_inline_evidence_cards`**: Previously, a cached `data/munger/computed/<TICKER>.json` report's stale `bucket` (e.g. `watch`) could shadow a newer `watchlist.json` bucket (`core`). `resolve_inline_evidence_cards` now always prefers live `wl_entry.bucket` (and non-empty `wl_entry.name`) from `workbench/watchlist.json` when present.

### 5.2 Async-Safe Connection Probe & Lightweight Feynman Runtime
- **`test_alan_connection` Tokio Runtime Safety (`crates/m3-core/src/alan_connection.rs`)**: When `test_with_paths` is called while already inside an active Tokio runtime (`tokio::runtime::Handle::try_current().is_ok()`), it spawns a dedicated `std::thread::Builder` OS thread to build and `block_on` a `current_thread` Tokio runtime instead of panicking with nested-runtime errors.
- **Fast Single-Pass Feynman Critique (`crates/m3-core/src/feynman.rs`, `alan_interrogator.rs`)**: Added `boot_m3_feynman_embedded` (`max_tool_loops = 1`, zero `m3_*` external tool loops) and synchronous `evaluate_feynman_round_blocking` (also safe inside/outside Tokio) so 3-round Socratic Feynman drills complete deterministically without timing out on redundant tool calls.

### 5.3 Stale Zombie `research_runs` Reclamation
- **Core Reclamation (`crates/m3-core/src/lake_l2.rs`, `research.rs`)**: Exported `DEFAULT_STALE_RESEARCH_RUN_MS = 15 * 60 * 1000` (15 minutes) and `LakeDb::reclaim_stale_running_runs(now_ms, stale_after_ms)`, marking abandoned `running` rows whose `now_ms.saturating_sub(started_at_ms) >= stale_after_ms` as `failed` with an explicit interrupted message and `finished_at_ms = now_ms`. `research::run_deep_research_with_llm` invokes this reclamation pre-flight.
- **CLI Visibility & Opportunistic Cleanup (`crates/m3-cli/src/lib.rs`)**: `m3 doctor` reports a `stale_research_runs` health check (`running_research_runs`, `stale_running_research_runs`), and `m3 research status` opportunistically opens RW DuckDB when free (gracefully falling back to RO if `M3.app` owns the lock) to reclaim stale runs and report `reclaimed_stale_runs`.

### 5.4 Synchronous Scoped Interrogation & `m3 alan` CLI
- **Blocking Scoped Helper (`crates/m3-core/src/alan_interrogator.rs`)**: Exported `ask_scoped_blocking` / `ask_scoped_blocking_with_llm` returning `ScopedAlanReply` (`scope`, `scope_key`, `prose`, `turing_tape`, `evidence_tokens`, `evidence_cards`, `session_path`, `input_tokens`, `output_tokens`, `elapsed_ms`). Events are correlated strictly by the active `submission_id` and persisted atomically to `workbench/alan_sessions/<scope_key>.json`.
- **`m3 alan` & Enhanced `m3 doctor`**: Added `m3 alan status`, `m3 alan test [--profile <ID>]`, `m3 alan ask --scope <SCOPE> "<QUESTION>" [--lang zh|en] [--timeout <SECS>]`, and `m3 alan session <show|clear> --scope <SCOPE>`, plus an `alan_connection` health check (with non-secret `masked_hint` and actionable remediation) inside `m3 doctor`.

### 5.5 Full-Capability `m3 mcp` (`40` Tools + `--read-only`)
- **`crates/m3-cli/src/mcp.rs`**: Expanded `m3 mcp` from 17 read-only tools to **40 tools** (**24 read-only** + **16 controlled action, Alan, and desktop-control tools**), all carrying `destructiveHint: false` and accurate `readOnlyHint`, `idempotentHint`, and `openWorldHint` metadata. Passing `--read-only` filters `tools/list` back to the 24 read-only tools and rejects action calls with `-32602`. See [docs/mcp-server.md](mcp-server.md) for the full catalog.

### 5.6 `M3.app` Live Desktop Control IPC (`control.sock` & `m3 app`)
- **Core IPC Protocol (`crates/m3-core/src/app_control.rs`)**: Defines `control_socket_path()` (`M3_CONTROL_SOCKET` env override or `<M3Paths::config_dir>/control.sock`), `AppControlRequest` (`Ping`, `GetState`, `Navigate`, `SetAlan`, `TriggerAction`, `Screenshot`), `AppControlState`, `AppControlResponse`, and `send_app_control_request` / `send_app_control_request_at` over Unix domain sockets.
- **Desktop Server (`apps/m3-nsh/src/control_ipc.rs`)**: `start_control_server(store)` binds `control.sock` with `0600` permissions on a background OS thread (safely cleaning up stale unconnected socket files without clobbering live instances) and dispatches requests onto the NSH engine thread (`dispatch_app_control_request`). On macOS, `Screenshot` discovers the process window ID via CoreGraphics and writes a clean PNG via `screencapture -l <wid> -o -x`.
- **CLI & MCP Surface**: Exposed both through `m3 app <ping|state|navigate|alan|action|screenshot>` and through `m3_app_state`, `m3_app_navigate`, `m3_app_alan`, `m3_app_action`, and `m3_app_screenshot`.
